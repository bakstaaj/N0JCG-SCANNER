# PI Scanner v1.0.10

- Adds Squelch −/+, Skip, Block, and Clear beside the ON AIR status.
- Controls remain visible during P25 traffic but are disabled.
- Controls enable automatically when VHF or UHF owns the audio.
- Skip suppresses the identified channel for 10 minutes.
- Block suppresses the identified channel until cleared.
- Clear removes skips and blocks for the active analog band.
- Squelch changes by 100 RMS per press.
- Worker controls are applied live without service restarts.
